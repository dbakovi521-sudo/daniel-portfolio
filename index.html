About

Daniel Bakovic is an architectural designer based in Serbia, with nearly four years of professional experience across architectural design, technical development and construction documentation.

His experience spans retail, commercial, public and residential projects, from early concept design through detailed development and construction documentation.

He has contributed to international projects and competitions across Europe and beyond, with a particular focus on BIM workflows, façade development, technical detailing and multidisciplinary coordination.

His approach combines architectural quality with technical precision, buildability and clear communication throughout the design process.
